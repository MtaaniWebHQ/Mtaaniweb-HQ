Advertising and Sponsored Content Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

MtaaniWeb is supported by businesses that pay for promotion. This policy explains how paid content is handled so that users can always tell it apart from editorial content.

1. Clear labelling

Every sponsored placement, campaign post and paid feature is labelled as sponsored. Labels are never hidden or removed.

2. Editorial independence

Mtaani Picks, guides and editorial recommendations are chosen by MtaaniWeb. A business that pays for promotion does not receive an editorial pick, a guide mention or a verification badge as part of that payment.

3. Where sponsored content appears

Sponsored placements appear on clearly marked surfaces such as category pages, area pages, discovery listings and sponsored feed positions.

4. Standards for advertisers

Paid content must be truthful, lawful and consistent with our Content Guidelines. We may refuse or remove any campaign that is misleading, offensive or harmful.

5. Labels are independent

Verified, Claimed, Featured and Sponsored are independent labels. Paying for a campaign never makes a business Verified.

6. Your own advertising accounts

Where advertising runs on Meta, TikTok or Google, the advertising budget is separate from MtaaniWeb's service fee, and you should keep ownership of your accounts where possible.

7. Questions

To ask about advertising, use the Contact page. See also our Campaign Terms.
