About MtaaniWeb

MtaaniWeb is a Kenyan local discovery platform helping you find places, events, experiences and things happening around you.

Whether you're looking for a great restaurant, a new coffee spot, a place for date night, live music, a weekend activity or somewhere to go tonight, MtaaniWeb helps you discover what's worth checking out.

We bring local businesses, interesting places, events and experiences into one discovery experience, making it easier to explore your city and find something that fits your plans.

Discover more of your surroundings

Finding somewhere to go shouldn't mean scrolling endlessly through social media, asking everyone for recommendations or stumbling across places long after the moment has passed. MtaaniWeb brings discovery closer to you.

Explore restaurants, cafés, nightlife, events, shopping, wellness spots, outdoor activities and hidden gems. Browse by location, follow your interests, discover what's trending and find ideas for today, tonight or the weekend. From familiar neighbourhood favourites to places you haven't discovered yet, there's always something new to explore.

Made for the way Kenya moves

Kenya has a vibrant mix of places, people, businesses and experiences. New restaurants open, events happen, neighbourhoods change and different parts of the city come alive at different times. MtaaniWeb exists to help people keep up with that movement.

We're creating a discovery platform where local places and experiences are easier to find, explore and share, starting with Nairobi and expanding across Kenya.

How it works

MtaaniWeb is free to use for discovery. The Newsroom, MtaaniWeb's editorial team, curates what appears, programmed by time of day, day of week, and what is actually happening around Nairobi. What you see in the morning is not what you see at midnight.

Businesses can appear on MtaaniWeb for free as part of the discovery inventory. Additional visibility, sponsored placement and campaign distribution are commercial products, clearly labelled wherever they appear. Read more in our Advertising Policy.

Verification

A Mtaani Verified badge means MtaaniWeb has completed its verification process for that business. Paying for promotion never grants verification, and the two are always kept separate.

Helping local businesses get discovered

Behind every interesting place is a business, a team or someone with something worth sharing. MtaaniWeb gives businesses and venues another way to reach people actively looking for places to visit, things to do and experiences to enjoy. Through local discovery, editorial features and promotional campaigns, we help connect businesses with people who may be interested in what they offer.
