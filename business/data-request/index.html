Data Request and Deletion Process

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask what personal information MtaaniWeb holds about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. This page explains how.

What you can ask for

  • Access: a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: fixing information that is wrong or out of date
  • Deletion: removing your personal information, where the law allows
  • Objection: asking us to stop using your information for a purpose, such as marketing

How to make a request

  • Send your request through the Contact page and choose "Data request" as the topic, or email us at hello@mtaaniweb.co.ke.
  • Tell us your name and the email address or phone number you used with MtaaniWeb.
  • Say clearly what you want us to do.

Confirming who you are

To protect your information, we may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on a request.

What happens next

We will acknowledge your request promptly and respond within the time the law requires. If we cannot do what you ask, for example because the law requires us to keep a record, we will explain why.

Removing a business listing

If you represent a business and want its listing corrected or removed, use the same process and tell us the business name and your role.
